Analytics plan and dashboard demo · Prepared by Zeeshan · 24 October 2026

The problem

Venues pay every month. If they can’t see what VenueGPS brought them, they leave.

The one story we sell

“We put your venue in front of X people. Y of them took action. About Z showed up.”

So the plan is simple

Every number and every graph on the venue dashboard feeds this story. Anything that doesn’t, we leave out.

Where: Insights tab in the business account, plus a monthly email or PDF

VenueGPS Admin

For the VenueGPS team

Question: Is the app growing, converting and keeping venues?

Where: internal dashboard built on GA4, BigQuery and billing

Sees: all users, all venues, all revenue

Fresh: daily

Why not just give venues a GA4 login? GA4 can’t safely limit a login to one venue, it hides small numbers, and it can’t show redemptions or push sends. Our own dashboard can, and it becomes a feature we control and can charge for.

Why cut half the list?

A number that is always zero, or one an owner can’t act on, hurts trust. Fewer, honest numbers sell renewals better.

Also: daily refresh instead of real-time. It’s enough for owners and far cheaper.

Proving “people who came because of VenueGPS”

Three levels of proof, labelled and never mixed

Intent

“Planned to come”

Tapped Get Directions, a ticket link or Call.

Works today with app events.

Estimated

“Likely visited”

Viewed or saved the venue, then was inside its area within 7 days.

Needs opt-in background location.

Verified

“Confirmed visit”

Door offer redeemed, QR check-in, or ticket scanned at the door.

Needs venues to accept a door offer.

Fastest win: a “Show this at the door” VenueGPS offer. The app already has a 15% discount pop-up. Each redemption is a confirmed visit, and staff confirm it with one tap.

Privacy rules

Venues see counts, never people

Totals only

No names, emails or lists of viewers. Owners see a follower count, not who follows.

Groups of 10 or more

Any slice under 10 users shows as “fewer than 10”, so no one can be singled out.

City level only

Raw location stays on the server and is deleted after visits are matched.

Consent first

iOS tracking prompt, consent mode on web, background location opt-in only. The privacy policy says venues get aggregate stats.
