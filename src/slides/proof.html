Proving “people who came because of VenueGPS”

Three levels of proof, labelled and never mixed

Intent

“Planned to come”

Tapped Get Directions, a ticket link or Call.

Works today with app events.

Estimated

“Likely visited”

Viewed or saved the venue, then was inside its area within 7 days.

Needs opt-in background location.

Verified

“Confirmed visit”

Door offer redeemed, QR check-in, or ticket scanned at the door.

Needs venues to accept a door offer.

Fastest win: a “Show this at the door” VenueGPS offer. The app already has a 15% discount pop-up. Each redemption is a confirmed visit, and staff confirm it with one tap.
