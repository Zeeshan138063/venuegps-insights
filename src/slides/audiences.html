For the VenueGPS team

Question: Is the app growing, converting and keeping venues?

Where: internal dashboard built on GA4, BigQuery and billing

Why not just give venues a GA4 login? GA4 can’t safely limit a login to one venue, it hides small numbers, and it can’t show redemptions or push sends. Our own dashboard can, and it becomes a feature we control and can charge for.
